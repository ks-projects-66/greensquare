# Cookie notice

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This notice explains how www.greensquare.ai, run by GreenSquare AI (the trading name of the operator of this site), uses cookies and similar technologies. Read it alongside our privacy policy.

## What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device. It lets the site work properly and remember basic technical details between pages.

## Which cookies we use

We keep cookies to a minimum. We do not run advertising cookies, and we do not use trackers that follow you across other websites.

- **Functional cookies.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, may set functional cookies so pages load and behave correctly.
- **Analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which sites link to us. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device, and does not follow you to other websites. We use it to understand which pages are useful, never to build a profile of you or to target advertising.

We do not set cookies to identify you personally. The only personal detail you give us is your email address, entered yourself on a form.

## Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser. Most browsers let you see what is stored, block cookies, or delete them. If you block functional cookies, some parts of the site and any available checkout may not work as intended.

## Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our use of cookies changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
